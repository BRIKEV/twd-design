# TWD Design for companies

TWD Design is open source and stays that way. Your dev team can set it up alone in about 15 minutes.

Rolling it out across a company takes more than installing it: a security review, a design guide that
makes the prototypes look like your product, and a team that knows how to agree on a screen in a thread.
If you would rather not do that work alone, write to
[hello@twd.dev](mailto:hello@twd.dev?subject=TWD%20Design%20for%20our%20company) and we do it with you.

## A pilot with one team

The best way to find out if it fits is to try it on real work. We pick one product team and one feature
together, run TWD Design on your infrastructure for a few weeks, and look at what changed: how long it took
to agree on the screen, and how much was lost in the handoff.

## What we can do with you

- **Rollout and security review.** TWD Design deployed on your servers, with your Slack app, GitHub App
  and the AI provider your company already approves. We go through
  [what is read, sent and stored](/docs/reference/security) with your security team.
- **Your design guide.** We write `design/DESIGN.md` with your designers: colors, type, components and
  tone, so every prototype looks like your product from the first version.
- **Training for the product trio.** How product, design and development ask for a screen, change it and
  agree on it in a thread, and what developers do with the pull request.
- **From the prototype to CI.** The acceptance tests that come with each prototype, running against the
  real app in your pipeline with [TWD](https://twd.dev).
- **Microsoft Teams.** TWD Design works in Slack today. If your company works in Teams, we adapt it to
  the way your teams use it.
- **Support with agreed response times.** A named contact, response times agreed in advance, and help
  with upgrades.
